In this lesson, your AI tutor will ask you a few optional questions about your background and preferences. Your answers help tailor the rest of the course to you.

Every fruit has layers: history, science, language, and culture. Knowing what you're most curious about helps your tutor emphasize the angles you'll enjoy most.

All questions are optional. Skip any you'd rather not answer.
